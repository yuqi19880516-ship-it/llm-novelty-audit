# Addendum 2: target-equivalent categories (applies to three targets only)

For three targets the broader category is, in the literature, effectively the same disease, so its records count exactly like records on the target disease (they can support L0):
- pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma: records on "pancreatic cancer" or "pancreatic adenocarcinoma" (ductal adenocarcinoma is about 90% of pancreatic cancers);
- idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis: records on pulmonary fibrosis, including the bleomycin (and similar) pulmonary-fibrosis models, which are the standard preclinical models of the disease;
- non-alcoholic steatohepatitis: records on non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) or metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, of which NASH is the progressive form.
Apply codebook v2 to all records shown (both the "same" and the "parent" lists) with this equivalence; the final label is L0, L1 or L2 as usual. Quote the deciding sentence and PMID.
